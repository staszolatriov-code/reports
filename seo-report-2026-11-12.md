## 3. Top 5 Highest-Impact Opportunities

### Opportunity 1: "[TICKER] Analyst Ratings" — Per-Ticker Analyst Hub Pages

**Why it matters:**  
"[TICKER] analyst ratings" searches are extremely high-volume, high-intent queries. Users at this stage are evaluating whether to buy, hold, or sell. They are prime candidates for TipRanks free registration and premium conversion. These queries exist for every publicly traded stock — representing tens of thousands of indexable URLs.

**Why StockAnalysis is beatable:**  
StockAnalysis shows a simple buy/hold/sell percentage and a mean price target. There is no analyst identity, no accuracy history, no quality weighting. A user who lands there learns almost nothing about whether the consensus is reliable or driven by a handful of perennial bulls.

**What TipRanks should build:**  
A dedicated analyst ratings module on every stock page (and potentially a standalone `/stocks/[ticker]/analyst-ratings/` URL) that includes: top-rated analyst calls with star ratings and historical accuracy, consensus breakdown weighted by analyst quality, distribution of price targets with 12-month track record, and a Smart Score summary. This page should be crawlable, indexable, and richly structured for featured snippets.

**TipRanks' specific differentiator:**  
Analyst star ratings (1–5 stars based on historical accuracy and return performance) are entirely proprietary. No other platform assigns quality scores to individual analysts at scale. This is the moat.
