### Opportunity 1: Per-Analyst Price Target Pages (Ticker-Level)

**Why it matters:**
"[TICKER] price target" and "[TICKER] analyst price target" are among the highest-volume financial queries for any major stock. NVDA, AAPL, TSLA, META — each generates tens of thousands of searches per month. These queries spike massively during earnings season (July right now), creating a time-sensitive capture window. Users on this query are investment-decision-ready.

**Why StockAnalysis is weak here:**
StockAnalysis renders a single consensus average (e.g., "$185 average target") with a list of analyst firm names and their targets — but no analyst identity metadata, no accuracy rating, no track record link, and no ability to filter by analyst quality. It's a table, not an insight.

**What TipRanks should build:**
Optimize existing stock pages to be the definitive "analyst price target" destination for every ticker. Each page should surface: (1) the Smart Score-weighted consensus, not a naive average, (2) individual analyst cards showing name, firm, success rate, and latest target, (3) a visual timeline of target changes over 12 months, (4) a clear CTA to see full analyst history (premium). Structured data markup (FinancialProduct, AnalysisNewsArticle schemas) to earn rich snippets in Google.

**TipRanks' specific differentiator:**
No other platform publicly shows analyst accuracy alongside their current price target. A target from an analyst with a 72% success rate means something very different from one with a 38% rate. This is TipRanks' unique value proposition — it belongs on the SEO surface, not just behind a paywall.
