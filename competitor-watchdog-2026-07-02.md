## MarketBeat.com

### 1. Summary of What Changed

**Quartr Partnership → Earnings Transcript Empire.** Announced July 2025, MarketBeat integrated the Quartr API to deliver live earnings call audio, real-time transcripts, earnings summaries, presentations, and news releases per company on a single page. Individual transcript pages now exist at `/earnings/reports/[date]-[company]-stock/` — a scalable programmatic template targeting every "[company] earnings call transcript Q[X] 202X" query.

**Yahoo Finance Syndication.** MarketBeat content now appears on Yahoo Finance (alongside existing MSN, Nasdaq, Investing.com distribution). Revenue approaching $50M (20% YoY growth) suggests accelerating investment in content and distribution.

**Homepage + Dashboard Rebuild.** "My MarketBeat" dashboard rebuilt from scratch with customizable watchlists. New homepage launched with improved internal linking. Screener modernization planned — speed, usability overhaul.

**Content Depth Expansion.** Two long-form research reports/month exclusive to All Access members. AI features help users interpret market news, earnings, and watchlist activity faster.

---

### 2. Why It Matters

- **Earnings transcripts + audio** is a high-intent, high-volume query cluster (e.g., "Apple earnings call transcript Q2 2026"). MarketBeat now has both text AND audio per-company earnings pages that TipRanks lacks at scale.
- **Yahoo Finance syndication** dramatically extends content reach. Articles can outrank MarketBeat.com's own pages OR create co-ranking that floods TipRanks out of top positions on brand + analysis queries.
- **$50M revenue / 20% growth** signals a well-funded competitor actively investing in SEO surface area. The screener overhaul will improve Core Web Vitals and usability scores.
- The **Dividend Screener** (updated Jan 2026) and **IPO Calendar** pages reinforce MarketBeat's dominance in evergreen finance tool queries where TipRanks has thin coverage.

### 4. Recommended Response

- **Build now:** Per-company earnings transcript pages with audio embed support. TipRanks already has analyst-tied earnings context — add transcript text + key quote extraction to create richer pages than MarketBeat's basic Quartr output.
- **Strengthen internal linking:** Audit TipRanks earnings calendar → analyst rating pages → transcript (if available) → price target chain. MarketBeat's rebuild is tightening this linkage.
- **Monitor:** Track SERP positions for "[stock ticker] earnings call transcript" queries monthly. If MarketBeat begins ranking top 3, escalate to Critical.
- **Improve conversion hooks** on earnings and dividend pages — MarketBeat's free-tier generosity is the entry drug; TipRanks' Smart Score is the differentiator to amplify.

## StockAnalysis.com

### 1. Summary of What Changed

**Analyst Pages Powered by TipRanks Data — and Ranking for TipRanks' Keywords.** StockAnalysis.com's `/analysts/` page uses TipRanks as its data provider (updated daily). They've built a `/analysts/top-stocks/` page listing the Top 50 Strong Buy stocks from 4-star+ analysts. These pages rank for "top wall street analysts" and "best analyst stock picks" — queries TipRanks should own.

**New Filings Pages per Company** (`/stocks/[ticker]/filings/`). Powered by Quartr, these add per-company pages with quarterly/annual reports, earnings call slides, proxy statements, and investor relations documents.

**Mutual Fund Screener** (`/tools/mutf-screener/`) — 81 indicators. New index page `/tools/` launched with: CAGR Calculator, Dividend Calculator, Stock Comparison, ETF Comparison, Fundamental Charts (beta).

**Brokerage Sync GA.** Pro users can sync brokerage holdings including options (puts/calls/LEAPs). Significant conversion driver.

**Sector/Industry PE Ratios** added to stock screener (299 total indicators). 52-Week High/Low Date data points added.

---

### 2. Why It Matters

- **TipRanks is providing data to a competitor that is capturing TipRanks' own organic keywords.** StockAnalysis's analyst pages rank for queries that should send users directly to TipRanks. Instead, TipRanks' data is feeding a free page that intercepts the traffic upstream.
- **The `/tools/` index page** signals a deliberate SEO expansion into calculator/tool queries ("CAGR calculator", "dividend calculator") — high-volume, low-competition utility keywords with strong backlink potential.
- **Per-company filings pages** create a new programmatic template that captures "[company] SEC filings", "[company] annual report", and "[company] earnings slides" queries — adding indexable depth per stock page.
- **Brokerage sync + 299 screener metrics** deepens the moat for power users, reducing churn from free to paid conversion funnels that compete with TipRanks Pro.
- **App Store launch (iOS + Android)** opens a mobile acquisition channel that can drive reviews, app-store SEO, and direct installs outside of Google organic.

Explore whether canonical attribution, no-index requirements, or contractual traffic controls can be enforced.
- **Build now:** A suite of financial calculator pages (CAGR, dividend yield, price target upside calculator, Smart Score explainer). StockAnalysis is winning utility keyword traffic that TipRanks is ignoring.
- **Add filings / investor relations documents** to TipRanks stock pages. StockAnalysis is building a competing Quartr-powered template; TipRanks should integrate similar IR document access natively.
- **Improve screener differentiation:** With 299 indicators, StockAnalysis is competing on data breadth. TipRanks should compete on insight quality — analyst accuracy scores, Smart Score breakdowns within screener results.
- **Monitor:** Track StockAnalysis SERP ranking for "top wall street analysts" and "[ticker] analyst ratings" monthly.

## Cross-Competitor Signals

**Both MarketBeat and StockAnalysis are now Quartr data partners.** Quartr is becoming the default backend for earnings transcripts, filings, and IR documents across the financial data ecosystem. TipRanks has no equivalent integration visible — this is a convergent threat: both competitors will have similar per-company IR pages ranking for the same filing/transcript queries.

**Programmatic page expansion is the shared playbook.** Both competitors are adding new URL templates (filings, transcripts, tools, screeners) to scale indexable pages without proportional editorial effort. TipRanks' page count and template variety needs to keep pace.

**Free-tier data depth is widening.** Both platforms are giving away more high-quality data for free. This lowers the barrier for users to satisfy research needs without reaching TipRanks' Smart Score or premium analyst layers — eroding the mid-funnel.

**Tool/calculator pages are an untapped SEO moat.** Neither TipRanks nor competitors have fully dominated this space yet. StockAnalysis is making the first serious move. First-mover advantage in utility keyword rankings could capture durable backlinks.
