# TipRanks SEO Page Builder — Hedge Fund Holdings

**Date:** 2026-09-16
**Selected cluster:** Hedge Fund Holdings (rotated from prior "analyst ratings")
**Why today:** Q2 2026 13F filings (deadline Aug 14) are freshly digested by markets, so search interest for "what did [manager] buy last quarter" peaks between late August and mid-September. This is a differentiated TipRanks strength (13F + institutional aggregation + expert track records) where the three named competitors are structurally weaker.

---

## 1. Page Thesis

A **top-level Hedge Fund Holdings hub** — not a blog post — targeting head-term queries ("hedge fund holdings", "top hedge funds", "13F filings tracker") that fans out to two SEO-critical templates: **/hedge-funds/[manager-slug]** (e.g., Bridgewater, Renaissance, Pershing Square) and **/stocks/[ticker]/hedge-fund-activity**. It's built for retail investors who want to mirror smart money without reading 400 raw 13F PDFs. It deserves to rank because it turns SEC filings into ranked, scored, cross-referenced data with TipRanks' proprietary "Hedge Fund Confidence Signal" on every ticker — something no competitor exposes. It converts because "which stocks are hedge funds buying right now" is a natural pathway into Smart Score, expert tracking, and Premium screener.

## 2. Search Intent Breakdown

- **Primary:** "Show me what top hedge funds are buying and selling right now" — transactional/investigational, wants a leaderboard + latest moves.
- **Secondary:** "Track a specific manager (Buffett, Ackman, Burry, Michael Burry, Cathie Wood) or a specific stock's institutional flow" — navigational to manager/stock pages.
- **What users really want:** A ranked, sortable list of positions with signal ("is this a new buy, add, or exit?"), performance context, and confidence — not a stale table of tickers.
- **Bounce triggers:** Data older than the last 13F cycle, no search/filter, no manager performance track record, gated behind a paywall above the fold, or a wall of raw filings text.

## 3. 10x Page Blueprint

- **Page type:** SEO hub page with three linked templates — (a) `/hedge-funds` hub, (b) `/hedge-funds/[manager]` profile, (c) `/stocks/[ticker]/hedge-fund-activity` per-ticker view.
- **Title tag (hub):** `Hedge Fund Holdings & 13F Tracker — Top Manager Portfolios | TipRanks`
- **Meta description:** `Track what the top 500 hedge funds are buying and selling. Live 13F holdings, manager track records, and TipRanks' proprietary Hedge Fund Confidence Signal on every stock.`
- **H1:** `Hedge Fund Holdings — What Top Managers Are Buying Now`
- **H2/H3 outline:**
  - H2 Top Hedge Funds This Quarter (leaderboard)
    - H3 By AUM · By 1Y return · By TipRanks Star Rating
  - H2 Most Bought Stocks (Q2 2026)
  - H2 Most Sold / Exited Stocks
  - H2 New Positions Across Top 50 Funds
  - H2 Search a Manager
  - H2 Search a Stock's Hedge Fund Activity
  - H2 How TipRanks Scores Hedge Fund Confidence (methodology)
  - H2 13F Filing Calendar (next deadline countdown)
  - H2 FAQ
- **Recommended modules:** Manager leaderboard (sortable), Most Bought / Sold heatmap, "New Buys" ticker chip cloud, Hedge Fund Confidence Signal explainer card, "Follow this manager" alert CTA, Smart Score cross-link on every ticker.
- **Interactive components:** Manager search-as-you-type; ticker filter that jumps to `/stocks/[ticker]/hedge-fund-activity`; time-slider (last 4 quarters); "compare 2 managers" side-by-side; watchlist add from any row.
- **Visual/data components:** Quarter-over-quarter position size delta bars; concentration donut (top 10 holdings % of AUM); manager track record sparklines; sector rotation heatmap across all filers; a "13F freshness" freshness badge on each row (green ≤ 45 days).
- **Schema opportunities:** `Dataset` on the hub, `Organization` per manager, `FinancialProduct` per stock cross-ref, `FAQPage`, `BreadcrumbList`, `ItemList` on leaderboards, `Article`/`Report` for the methodology page.
- **Internal linking strategy:** Every manager row links to `/hedge-funds/[manager]`; every ticker links to `/stocks/[ticker]/hedge-fund-activity`, which links up to `/stocks/[ticker]` (Smart Score) and sideways to `/stocks/[ticker]/analyst-ratings` and `/stocks/[ticker]/insider-trading`. Footer cluster: "Related smart-money tools" → Insider Trades, Analyst Ratings, Congressional Trading. Breadcrumb: Home → Experts → Hedge Funds → [Manager].

## 4. Differentiation vs. Competitors

**StockAnalysis.com**
- *Does:* Clean institutional-ownership table per stock, top-holders list.
- *Weak:* No per-manager profile depth, no track-record scoring, no cross-fund signal, no alerts.
- *TipRanks beats them:* Manager-level pages with track records, star ratings, and a live confidence score aggregated across filers.
- *Above the fold:* Hedge Fund Confidence Signal + top 3 manager moves on the ticker.

**Barchart.com**
- *Does:* Raw 13F filings table, ownership % columns, feature-gated behind Premier.
- *Weak:* Data-dense but hostile UX, no manager narrative, most useful views paywalled early.
- *TipRanks beats them:* Free ranked leaderboards + one clear signal; premium unlocks depth, not the basics.
- *Above the fold:* "Most Bought This Quarter" leaderboard with Smart Score column.

**MarketBeat.com**
- *Does:* Institutional Ownership pages built for SEO with heavy affiliate ad load.
- *Weak:* Ad-cluttered, slow, thin methodology, no proprietary score, no meaningful manager pages.
- *TipRanks beats them:* Cleaner UX, faster page, proprietary scoring, cross-linked expert ecosystem (analysts + insiders + bloggers + funds under one roof).
- *Above the fold:* Manager leaderboard with 1Y return and TipRanks Star Rating in the first viewport.

## 5. Conversion Strategy

- Above-the-fold CTA: "Follow [manager] — get alerted when they file" (free account gate).
- Row-level CTA: "Add [ticker] to watchlist" — one-click, free, hooks into Smart Score email.
- Free tier exposes: leaderboards, top 10 positions per manager, last-quarter deltas.
- Premium boundary: full 40+ position roster, quarter-over-quarter history beyond 4 quarters, custom alerts, "manager overlap" tool, downloadable CSV.
- Upgrade hook: "See all 63 positions →" contextual paywall on manager pages, priced against the specific value ("this manager has beaten the S&P by X% — see their full book").
- Trust elements: SEC filing timestamp + source link on every row, methodology page, TipRanks manager track record with sample-size disclosure.
- Engagement modules: 13F filing countdown widget, "New Buys This Week" email opt-in, comparison tool.
- Retention: personalized "Your followed managers just filed" email — the single highest-intent trigger this cluster can generate.

## 6. Editorial Guidance

- Tone: neutral, data-forward, no "guru worship" language — position TipRanks as the referee, not the fan.
- Depth: methodology and caveats visible (13F lag, long-only bias, no shorts/derivs) — this earns E-E-A-T and pre-empts skeptic bounces.
- Freshness cadence: hub headline stats refresh nightly; manager pages update within 24 h of each 13F filing; a visible "Last updated" timestamp everywhere.
- E-E-A-T signals: bylined analyst commentary on notable quarterly moves, links to underlying SEC filings, TipRanks editorial standards footer, cite the exact form (13F-HR, 13F-HR/A).
- Editorial calendar: quarterly "What top hedge funds bought" recap tied to each filing deadline (Feb 14 / May 15 / Aug 14 / Nov 14) — evergreen slugs, updated in place.
- Voice discipline: avoid "hedge funds are buying X — you should too". Frame as signal, never advice.
