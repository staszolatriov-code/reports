## 3. Top 5 Highest-Impact Opportunities

### Opportunity 1: Cluster Insider Buying Pages

**Why it matters:**
"Cluster buying" — when multiple insiders at the same company purchase shares within a short window — is one of the most statistically reliable signals of near-term outperformance. Retail investors are increasingly sophisticated about this signal, and search demand is growing. Queries like "cluster insider buying," "multiple insiders buying same stock," and "coordinated insider purchases" are underserved: most results are academic papers or shallow news articles.

**Why StockAnalysis is weak or beatable:**
StockAnalysis shows raw Form 4 data per ticker, but has zero algorithmic detection of cluster-buy patterns. They cannot surface this insight without building it from scratch. Their insider pages are essentially a reformatted SEC feed.

**What TipRanks should build:**
- A dedicated "Cluster Insider Buying" screener page with live data
- An evergreen explainer (1,500–2,000 words) on what cluster buying is, with embedded live screener widget
- Weekly "Cluster Insider Buys This Week" automated freshness page

**TipRanks' specific differentiator:**
TipRanks already detects and surfaces cluster-buy signals in the product — this is a matter of wrapping SEO-optimized landing pages around existing functionality, not building new features.
