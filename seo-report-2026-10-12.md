## 3. Top 5 Highest-Impact Opportunities

### Opportunity 1: Analyst-Accuracy-Ranked Stock Pages
**Why it matters:**  
"[TICKER] analyst price target" queries run into the millions per month during earnings season. Investors are not just looking for price targets — they want to know *which analyst to trust*. This is the exact gap TipRanks fills.

**Why StockAnalysis is weak:**  
StockAnalysis shows raw consensus numbers (avg price target, % upside) with no analyst credibility scoring. Their pages rank on authority and fundamentals coverage, not data depth. Every page is easy to out-differentiate with richer analyst-layer content.

**What TipRanks should build:**  
Ensure every stock detail page has a crawlable, richly structured analyst consensus module: analyst name, firm, star rating, track record (% correct, average return), most recent rating + price target, and a visual distribution of targets. Add FAQ schema ("What do analysts think about AAPL?", "What is the average analyst price target for AAPL?").

**TipRanks' specific differentiator:**  
The accuracy-ranked analyst system — no other public platform publishes analyst track records at this granularity. This is a defensible, link-worthy asset.
