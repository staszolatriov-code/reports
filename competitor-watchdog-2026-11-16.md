## MarketBeat.com

### 1. Summary of What Changed
- **Financial calculators expansion (Sept 2026):** Launched a dedicated `/calculators/` hub covering Debt-to-Equity, P/E Ratio, RSI, profit margin, rate of return, Roth IRA, and more. These are standalone, indexable tool pages.
- **Audio listening for articles (early 2026):** Added text-to-audio on stock market articles — a UX upgrade that reduces bounce and may improve dwell time signals.
- **Enhanced earnings + insider screeners (June 2026):** New research features added to both screeners, in partnership with Quartr and fiscal.ai. Still paywalled (All Access), but content around them is indexable.
- **"My MarketBeat" dashboard rebuild:** Customizable watchlists and dashboard now available free — lowers friction to account creation, feeds retention loop.
- **Distribution partnerships:** Approaching $50M revenue, signals aggressive off-site distribution, which may generate high-DA backlinks and referral traffic.

### 2. Why It Matters
The calculator expansion is a high-impact SEO move. Queries like "how to calculate P/E ratio" and "RSI calculator" have millions of monthly searches and are dominated by thin tools. MarketBeat is building a full tool hub to claim this traffic. TipRanks has no equivalent calculator library — this is a gap. The Quartr partnership improves earnings content depth, raising the bar on a high-intent category TipRanks also competes in.

| Finding | Threat Level |

| Earnings screener depth | **Medium** — paywalled, but indexable content strengthens topical authority |

| Distribution partnerships | **Medium** — could yield backlink spikes |

### 4. Recommended Response
- **Build a financial calculator hub** mirroring or exceeding MarketBeat's depth — these are product-led SEO pages TipRanks can win with real data integration.
- **Refresh earnings-related landing pages** to match new content depth signals.
- **Monitor** Quartr-sourced earnings pages for new keyword rankings monthly.

## Cross-Competitor Signals

Both MarketBeat and StockAnalysis are converging on the same strategy: **tool-led SEO**. They are building calculators, screeners, and interactive tools that earn links, reduce bounce, and target long-tail queries that static editorial content cannot capture. Both are also layering AI features onto paid tiers.

The other shared pattern is **data depth over breadth**: adding more columns, filters, and formula-based metrics to existing pages rather than creating new URL structures. This makes their existing pages stronger without diluting domain authority.

TipRanks risks being outflanked in the **utility/tool layer** of financial search while competing mainly on analyst ratings — a defensible but narrower moat.
