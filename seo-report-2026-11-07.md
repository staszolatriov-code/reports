Each stock has a stable, recurring audience. These pages scale programmatically across TipRanks' entire coverage universe — potentially 10,000+ SEO-optimised pages from a single template.

StockAnalysis shows a raw table of SEC 4 filings with no analysis, no trend visualisation, no scoring, and no contextualisation. The page has minimal content depth and low time-on-site signals, making it vulnerable to displacement by a richer page.

A dedicated `/insider-trading/` tab (or standalone URL) for each stock featuring: interactive buy/sell timeline chart, insider identity breakdown (CEO vs CFO vs board), Smart Score correlation panel ("insiders are buying while Smart Score = 8"), and a plain-English signal summary ("3 insiders bought $4.2M in the last 30 days — historically bullish for this stock").

**TipRanks' specific differentiator:**
