## 3. Top 5 Highest-Impact Opportunities

### Opportunity 1: Analyst-Specific Landing Pages at Scale

**Why it matters:**
Queries like "JPMorgan analyst NVDA price target," "Goldman Sachs AAPL price target," or "analyst [name] track record" are high-intent, underserved, and directly tied to TipRanks' core data asset. Investors actively research which analysts to trust before acting — this is the final mile before a trade decision and before a premium subscription.

**Why StockAnalysis is weak:**
StockAnalysis lists aggregated price targets on stock pages but has no analyst identity layer. There are no individual analyst pages, no accuracy scores, no sector rankings by analyst. The intent gap is wide open.

**What TipRanks should build:**
- `/analysts/[analyst-name]/` pages with career stats, top-rated calls, sector specialization, and a feed of recent ratings
- Internal links from every stock page to the top analysts covering that stock
- A hub at `/top-analysts/` with filters by sector, accuracy tier, and time window

**TipRanks' specific differentiator:**
Analyst star ratings (1–5) and success rate percentages already exist in the product. These just need to be exposed as crawlable, structured pages with schema markup (Person schema + FinancialProduct schema for each rating).
