### Opportunity 1: "[Ticker] Analyst Price Target" Programmatic Pages

**Why it matters:**
"[Ticker] analyst price target" is searched millions of times monthly across the S&P 500 alone. These are high-intent users evaluating whether to buy or hold a stock — exactly the audience that converts to premium. The query exists for virtually every ticker in existence (NVDA, AAPL, TSLA, AMD, etc.), making it a programmatic SEO gold mine with compounding returns.

**Why StockAnalysis is weak here:**
StockAnalysis shows a consensus number but provides zero analyst credibility context — no breakdown of which analysts are rated 5-star vs. 1-star, no historical accuracy of the target-setters, no explanation of what the consensus distribution looks like (8 buys vs. 2 sells). It's a number with no intelligence behind it.

**What TipRanks should build:**
Dedicated, structured `/stocks/[ticker]/analyst-price-targets` pages that render: (1) current consensus price target with upside %, (2) breakdown of analyst ratings by conviction level, (3) each analyst's accuracy score and historical performance on this ticker, (4) price target trend over 12 months, (5) top analyst pick/sell signals. Gate the analyst accuracy layer behind a free registration to build email list.

**TipRanks' specific differentiator:**
Analyst accuracy ratings — the ranked track record of each analyst — are entirely proprietary. No scraper can replicate this. StockAnalysis and Yahoo Finance show the same consensus number; only TipRanks can show *which* analysts have the best track record on this specific stock.
