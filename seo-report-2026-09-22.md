### Opportunity 1: "Most Bought Stocks by Hedge Funds" — Live Ranking Page

**Why it matters**
After every 13F filing cycle (February, May, August, November) there is a measurable spike in searches for which stocks institutions are piling into. This intent is recurring, high-volume (~40K–90K monthly searches peak cycle), and strongly correlates with users who convert to premium to get alerts before the next cycle.

**Why StockAnalysis is weak here**
StockAnalysis shows raw institutional ownership percentages buried in individual stock pages. They have no aggregated "top hedge fund buys" view, no trend-over-time visualization, and no filtering by manager type (activist, quant, long-only). Their pages are data dumps, not insight pages.

**What TipRanks should build**
A dynamically updated, SEO-optimized page: `/hedge-funds/top-stocks/` that ranks the top 25 stocks by net new hedge fund buyers in the most recent 13F cycle. Include:
- Filter by sector, market cap, Smart Score minimum
- "Also insider-bought" badge where applicable
- Inline chart: hedge fund ownership % change over 4 quarters
- CTA: "Get alerts when hedge funds change positions" → premium upsell

**TipRanks' specific differentiator**
TipRanks already aggregates 13F data. The moat is the ability to layer Smart Score on top of hedge fund concentration — a combination no competitor can offer. A stock in the top-25 hedge fund buy list with Smart Score ≥ 8 is a uniquely TipRanks concept.

### Opportunity 2: "Insider Buying Stocks 2026" — Screener SEO Page

**Why it matters**
Insider buying is one of the most searched stock screening criteria among active retail investors. Monthly search volume for terms in this cluster exceeds 80K globally. Users searching these terms convert to paid products at roughly 2–3× the rate of users coming from generic "stocks to buy" queries, because they are explicitly looking for edge — which is exactly what a premium subscription sells.

**Why StockAnalysis is weak here**
StockAnalysis has a basic insider transactions table (sourced from SEC Form 4 filings) but it is a raw data table with no analysis layer. No sentiment signal, no aggregation by significance (large vs. small buys), no correlation with price performance, no comparison against the insider's own history.

**What TipRanks should build**
A dual-purpose page at `/insider-trading/top-buys/`:
- Auto-updated weekly list of the most significant insider buys (filtered by purchase size, % of shares outstanding, C-suite vs. director)
- Embed TipRanks' insider analyst reliability data: "This CEO's buys have preceded 12-month gains 73% of the time"
- Cluster supporting pages: sector-specific insider buying (tech insider buys, energy insider buys), and a definitional guide "What is insider buying?"
- Internal link to Smart Score pages for each stock

**TipRanks' specific differentiator**
TipRanks has historical accuracy data on insider traders — not just the transactions but the track record of those individuals. No other retail-facing platform has this at scale. This is the core product differentiator that should anchor every piece of content in this cluster.
