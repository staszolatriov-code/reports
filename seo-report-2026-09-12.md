## 3. Top 5 Highest-Impact Opportunities

### Opportunity 1: "Best Stocks Insiders Are Buying" — Screener + Editorial Hub

**Why it matters**  
This query family ("insiders buying stock," "best insider trading stocks," "stocks with insider buying") drives hundreds of thousands of monthly searches globally. Intent is squarely commercial-investigation — the user wants to act, not just read. Conversion rates on this traffic are high because the visitor already believes in the signal.

**Why StockAnalysis is weak**  
StockAnalysis shows raw insider transaction tables pulled from SEC Form 4 filings. There is no scoring, no context about whether an insider has a historically accurate predictive track record, no aggregation across multiple insiders at the same company, and no integration with other signals. It is a data dump, not a tool.

**What TipRanks should build**  
A dedicated SEO landing page at `/insider-trading/best-stocks-insiders-are-buying/` that:
- Leads with a dynamic, weekly-updated list of the top 20 stocks ranked by Insider Confidence Score
- Includes a filterable screener (by sector, market cap, insider role: CEO/CFO vs. director)
- Shows Smart Score alongside insider signal so visitors see corroborating evidence
- Requires free signup to access full list beyond top 5 (conversion gate)

**TipRanks' specific differentiator**  
The Insider Confidence Score is proprietary — it weights transactions by historical accuracy of the insider, not just transaction size. No competitor has this. Showing *which* insiders have a track record of profitable open-market purchases is a defensible, premium-feeling feature that directly maps to the visitor's intent.
