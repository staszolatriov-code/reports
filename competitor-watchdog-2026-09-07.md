## MarketBeat.com

### 1. Summary of What Changed

- **13F Filings + Congressional Trading Pages**: MarketBeat launched a full suite of institutional tracking pages — 13F filer holdings, congressional stock trades filtered by state, trade size, sector, and time period. These are high-intent, low-competition keyword clusters.
- **DividendStocks.com Satellite Site**: MarketBeat spun up a separate domain (dividendstocks.com) targeting dividend-specific queries, effectively doubling their topical authority surface for dividend keywords without cannibalizing MarketBeat.com.
- **Audio Listening Feature**: Article-level audio playback added — likely a UX/engagement signal play that could reduce bounce rates and improve dwell time (soft ranking signal).
- **Compare Stocks Tool**: A side-by-side stock comparison feature is now live. High conversion-intent query type.
- **"50 Best Dividend Stocks" Updated List**: Daily-refreshed best dividend stocks list — freshness signal targeting evergreen transactional queries like "best dividend stocks 2026."
- **Earnings Screener (All Access gated)**: New screener specifically for earnings announcements, deepening their screener ecosystem.
- **Screener pages updated**: Dividend screener, ETF screener, and stock screener all confirmed updated as of May 2026.

### 2. Why It Matters

- **Congressional/13F pages** directly compete with TipRanks' hedge fund holdings and smart money features. MarketBeat is now free-tier indexed for "who is buying [TICKER]" and "Congress stocks" queries.
- **DividendStocks.com** is a direct product-led SEO attack. A separate domain means MarketBeat can own two SERP slots for dividend queries.
- **Compare Stocks** targets bottom-funnel queries ("AAPL vs MSFT stock") that convert at high rates — TipRanks has limited coverage here.
- **Audio + dwell time improvements** could improve MarketBeat's Core Web Vitals signals indirectly.

### 3. Threat Level

| Finding | Threat Level |
|---|---|
| Congressional Trading / 13F pages | **High** |
| DividendStocks.com satellite site | **High** |
| Compare Stocks tool | **Medium** |
| Earnings Screener (gated) | **Medium** |

| Updated dividend/ETF screeners | **Medium** |

### 4. Recommended Response

- **Congressional/13F**: Build or strengthen TipRanks' "Smart Money" and "Hedge Fund Activity" pages. Add congressional trading data as a standalone page cluster. Improve internal linking from stock pages to smart money signals.
- **DividendStocks.com**: TipRanks should deepen dividend content — yield history, ex-dividend date trackers, dividend safety scores — to compete at the keyword level MarketBeat is now targeting with a dedicated domain.
- **Compare Stocks**: Build a comparison page template (`/compare/AAPL-vs-MSFT/`) targeting "[Stock A] vs [Stock B]" queries. These pages are high CTR, high conversion.
- **Screeners**: Ensure TipRanks' screener is indexed and has strong internal linking from stock/ETF/dividend pages.

## Cross-Competitor Signals

**Both MarketBeat and StockAnalysis are converging on the same playbook:**

1. **Screener depth**: Both are aggressively adding filters to screeners (earnings timing, CAGR, dividend years, moving averages). Screeners are becoming SEO moats — hundreds of filter combination URLs that rank for long-tail queries.

2. **Dividend content arms race**: Both have launched new dividend-specific pages (MarketBeat: DividendStocks.com + best dividend lists; StockAnalysis: Weekly/Monthly Dividend ETF lists, Dividend Payment Years). Dividend keyword clusters are a key battlefield.

3. **Institutional/Smart Money data**: MarketBeat added 13F + Congressional data. This is the "who's buying?" intent cluster — one of the highest conversion queries in stock research.

4. **Free-tier data depth vs. paywalls**: Both are giving more away for free to build traffic, then upselling premium (MarketBeat All Access, StockAnalysis Pro). TipRanks' paywall-heavy model may be losing click-through on informational queries where competitors now provide free depth.

5. **Page speed as a moat**: StockAnalysis' 50% load time improvement signals a product-led performance push. Speed is becoming a competitive differentiator, not just a technical hygiene item.
