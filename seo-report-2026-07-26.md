### Opportunity 1: Per-Ticker Insider Activity Pages at Scale

**Why it matters:**
"[TICKER] insider trading" and "[TICKER] insider buying" are among the most consistently searched stock research queries on Google. These searches spike whenever a stock moves on unusual volume or when executives make large purchases. There are tens of thousands of rankable ticker-specific queries. At scale, this is a traffic volume comparable to analyst rating pages — with higher commercial intent.

**Why StockAnalysis is beatable:**
StockAnalysis pulls raw Form 4 data directly from SEC EDGAR and presents it as a sortable table: date, insider name, transaction type, shares, price. There is no scoring, no sentiment signal, no trend line, no "what this means" layer. A user landing on StockAnalysis' insider page must interpret raw data themselves — which most retail investors cannot do confidently. That friction is an exit door.

**What TipRanks should build:**
Upgrade every per-ticker insider activity page to lead with the aggregated Insider Sentiment Score prominently above the fold, with a plain-English interpretation ("Insiders are net buyers over the past 3 months — historically bullish signal for NVDA"). Show trend sparklines for insider buy/sell ratio over time. Add a "cluster buying" alert badge when 3+ insiders buy within 30 days. Include the top 3 insiders by accuracy with their track record. Optimize H1 and title tags for "[TICKER] insider trading," "[TICKER] insider buying," and "[TICKER] insider activity."

**TipRanks' specific differentiator:**
The Insider Sentiment Score — a proprietary, aggregated signal that turns dozens of individual transactions into a single actionable number. StockAnalysis has no equivalent and cannot build one without raw transaction history + scoring infrastructure. TipRanks already has both.

### Opportunity 2: "Insider Buying Today" Freshness Feed Pages

**Why it matters:**
Active traders search for "insider buying today," "insider trades this week," and "stocks insiders are buying" on a daily basis. These are transactional-informational queries where freshness is a direct ranking factor. Google often surfaces a News Box or Top Stories carousel for these terms, meaning a page that updates daily can earn above-the-fold SERP real estate typically reserved for editorial publishers — without being a news site.

**Why StockAnalysis is beatable:**
StockAnalysis has no dedicated "today's insider trades" landing page with SEO optimization. Their insider section is a searchable table, not an indexed freshness feed. They do not publish daily summaries, do not have structured data markup for news content, and are not in Google News. Their page is built for power users who already know to look there — not for Google Discovery traffic.

**What TipRanks should build:**
A dedicated, auto-refreshing landing page at `/insider-trading/today` (and `/this-week`, `/this-month`) that: (a) lists the day's most significant insider buys with ticker, insider name, dollar amount, and TipRanks Insider Sentiment Score; (b) adds a brief editorial headline for the top 3 moves (eligible for Google News indexing); (c) publishes consistently every trading day; (d) uses schema markup for `ItemList` and `NewsArticle`. This page should be cross-linked from the TipRanks homepage and market movers section.

**TipRanks' specific differentiator:**
Daily publication cadence + scoring layer + editorial framing = a page Google will treat as a media property, not just a database. StockAnalysis cannot match the editorial layer without building a content team. TipRanks can automate 80% of this with template-driven copy built on live data.

## 4. Recommended Next Moves (Ordered by Impact)

1. **Audit all existing per-ticker insider pages** — identify which ones rank on page 2–3 for "[TICKER] insider trading" queries and push them to page 1 with on-page optimization: title tag updates, H1 alignment, Insider Sentiment Score prominence, and internal linking from the main stock page.

3. **Build individual insider profile pages for top 500 insiders** — prioritize Fortune 500 CEOs, known activist investors, and serial purchasers with strong historical accuracy. These pages will capture branded search traffic that currently goes to EDGAR or news sites.

4. **Add "Cluster Insider Buying" badge and filtering** — when 3+ insiders at the same company buy within 30 days, surface a prominent badge on the stock page and add the stock to a filterable "Cluster Buying" list. Optimize the list page for "stocks with cluster insider buying" queries.

5. **Create a Sector Insider Activity hub** — start with the 5 most-searched sectors (Technology, Healthcare, Energy, Financials, Consumer Discretionary). Each page should aggregate net buy/sell ratio and surface top stocks. Link the hub from the TipRanks Insider Trading main page.

6. **Publish original research: "How predictive is corporate insider buying?"** — use TipRanks' historical data to calculate average stock returns 30/60/90 days after clustered insider buying vs. individual purchases vs. no insider activity. Publish as a data study. This earns backlinks, press mentions, and demonstrates E-E-A-T that no competitor can match.

7. **Build the "Hedge Fund + Insider Overlap" screener and landing page** — this is the single highest-conversion page type given the dual proprietary data advantage. Optimize for premium upsell placement on the page (the user is already in a high-conviction research mindset).

8. **Implement internal linking from News/Sentiment pages to Insider pages** — TipRanks' news sentiment and blog sentiment pages are likely already indexed. Add contextual links from stories about insider activity to the relevant stock's insider page and any referenced insider profile pages.

9. **Schema markup audit for all insider-related pages** — ensure `Person` schema on insider profile pages, `Dataset` schema on screener/feed pages, and `BreadcrumbList` schema across the insider section. These are low-effort, high-impact signals for Google's understanding of TipRanks as an authority in this space.

10. **Defensive: claim "Form 4 explained" educational content** — StockAnalysis and generic finance sites currently own top rankings for educational insider trading queries. TipRanks should publish a comprehensive guide (with tool integration) to capture top-of-funnel users before they ever reach a competitor, then route them into the TipRanks insider data experience.
