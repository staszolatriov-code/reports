## MarketBeat.com

### 1. Summary of What Changed

**Dashboard redesign (Dec 2025):** Unified watchlists, portfolio holdings, alerts, news, and upcoming events into a single "My MarketBeat" dashboard. Free users can now track up to 10 holdings (doubled). Smoother cross-watchlist navigation and improved alert visibility.

**Audio articles (March 2026):** In-article text-to-audio player rolled out across all editorial content. No download or separate app required. Framed as accessibility but functions as a session-time and engagement driver.

**Momentum Alerts + Live News Feed (Q2 2026):** New All Access features targeting short-term traders. Signals that MarketBeat is competing harder for active-trader query segments.

**Enhanced screeners (June 2026):** Earnings and insider trades screeners received capability upgrades. Earnings screener filters by EPS, surprise history, revenue growth. Paid screeners (ETF, ratings, insider) remain gated at $29/month.

**Quartr partnership:** Live earnings-call audio now available through MarketBeat. High-intent feature for earnings-season traffic.

**Idea Engine:** Weekly AI-guided stock picks delivered via email and SMS for All Access subscribers.

**Organic traffic:** ~7.48M visits (Aug 2026), down -1.82% MoM — slight decline but volume remains dominant.

---

### 2. Why It Matters

- The dashboard redesign and audio features are UX moats that increase time-on-site and reduce bounce. TipRanks' analyst-first pages can feel static by comparison.
- Momentum Alerts signals MarketBeat is moving up-funnel toward higher-converting short-term-trader queries (stock alerts, buy signals) where TipRanks also competes.
- Quartr partnership gives MarketBeat live earnings-call coverage — a content type that generates substantial search volume every earnings season.
- Despite slight MoM traffic decline, MarketBeat holds the largest absolute traffic of the three (~7.5M), setting the benchmark TipRanks must chase.

### 4. Recommended Response

- **Momentum Alerts / trader signals:** Build or strengthen TipRanks' own stock alert product visibility. Ensure "stock buy signals," "momentum stocks," and "short-term trading picks" landing pages exist and are internally linked.
- **Dashboard/engagement:** Review TipRanks' logged-in dashboard experience. If it lacks a consolidated view (watchlist + analyst ratings + earnings + news), this is a churn risk. Prioritize a unified dashboard roadmap.
- **Audio:** Monitor adoption. If MarketBeat reports dwell-time improvements, TipRanks should consider a similar feature. No immediate action needed — monitor Q1 2027.
- **Earnings audio (Quartr):** Ensure TipRanks' earnings-call pages are optimized and internally linked from forecast/EPS pages. Compete on structured earnings data depth rather than raw audio.

## StockAnalysis.com

### 1. Summary of What Changed

**Charts tab in screener (October 2026):** Every stock in screener results now shows a price chart inline. 11 chart types, 3 timeframes (daily/weekly/monthly), studies including RSI, MACD, Bollinger Bands, and volume. This is a significant UX upgrade to what was a purely tabular screener.

**Revenue by segment and geography (October 2026):** Revenue pages now break down by business segment and geographic region with pie charts, 3-year history, TTM data, and quarterly views. Full segment history gated behind Pro. NVIDIA used as showcase example.

**Per-employee charts (October 2026):** Employee pages now show revenue-per-employee and similar productivity ratios charted over time.

**DCF Calculator + Fair Value Calculator (September 2026):** Free tools that compute intrinsic value from projected free cash flows. Positive FCF companies eligible; banks/insurers directed to Fair Value Calculator. These tools target high-intent "is [stock] undervalued" queries and generate calculator-specific landing pages.

**Fund Holdings Overlap tool (September 2026):** Shows overlap between ETF or mutual fund holdings. Targets fund comparison and ETF research queries — an underserved SEO segment.

**S&P Global financial forecasts (June 2026):** Analyst estimate data from S&P Global now rolling out globally. Directly competes with TipRanks' analyst consensus pages.

**Volume alerts for all logged-in users (July 2026):** Previously a paid feature. Free gating removed — expands retention and login incentive.

**Portfolio email/push summaries (October 2026):** Daily/weekly/monthly digests for portfolios. Increases return visit rates.

**Organic traffic:** Sep 2026: ~5.66M visits (+15.63% MoM recovery after a -12.74% MoM dip in January). Net trajectory: volatile but growing.

---

### 2. Why It Matters

- The **screener charts tab** is a major SEO and UX leap. Visual screeners rank well for "best stocks to buy" and "top dividend stocks" type queries. TipRanks' screener by comparison may feel data-heavy without visual context.
- **Revenue segment pages** create dozens of new indexable, data-rich URL paths per stock ticker — directly competing with TipRanks' stock analysis pages on long-tail revenue/segment queries.
- **DCF and Fair Value calculators** target "intrinsic value of [stock]" queries explicitly. These are high-intent, often transactional queries. If TipRanks lacks these tools, it loses clicks on a growing intent cluster.
- **S&P Global forecasts** directly challenge TipRanks' analyst consensus coverage. If users can get institutional-grade forecasts free on StockAnalysis, the value proposition of TipRanks' consensus data weakens.
- **Fund Holdings Overlap tool** attacks ETF-focused search traffic. This is a gap TipRanks may not have covered.
- The 15.63% MoM traffic recovery in September signals that StockAnalysis is accelerating — not stalling.

## Cross-Competitor Signals

Both MarketBeat and StockAnalysis are converging on the same playbook:

1. **Free tools as SEO pages** — calculators (DCF, fair value), screeners, overlap tools. Each tool generates a distinct landing page that ranks on intent-specific queries. TipRanks risks falling behind if it treats tools as app features rather than SEO assets.

2. **Deeper data per ticker** — both are expanding the number of data dimensions visible on stock pages (revenue segments, per-employee metrics, analyst forecasts). This creates more indexable depth per URL and targets more long-tail queries per ticker.

3. **Engagement loops for logged-in users** — both launched or expanded notification systems (portfolio summaries, volume alerts, Momentum Alerts). The goal is daily return visits. TipRanks should ensure it has comparable push/email engagement for free-tier users.

4. **Audio and visual enhancements** — MarketBeat's audio articles and StockAnalysis's chart-in-screener both signal that text-only pages are losing ground. Users (and Google's engagement signals) reward richer media.

**What this means for TipRanks strategy:** The battleground is shifting from "who has more data" to "who makes data more findable, visual, and actionable." TipRanks' analyst-ratings moat is real but must be defended actively, particularly as StockAnalysis adds S&P Global forecasts at no cost.
