## 3. Top 5 Highest-Impact Opportunities

### Opportunity 1 — "[TICKER] Insider Transactions" Pages at Scale

**Why it matters:**  
Per-stock insider transaction pages receive consistent navigational + informational traffic at massive scale. Every publicly traded ticker is a rankable URL. At 8,000+ US-listed stocks, even modest average monthly traffic per page compounds enormously. These pages capture investors already researching a specific stock — high commercial intent, short path to conversion.

**Why StockAnalysis is weak here:**  
StockAnalysis shows raw SEC insider transaction tables with minimal interpretation. It presents data, not signals. There is no sentiment layer, no Smart Score integration, no context about whether a cluster of buys historically preceded price moves for this stock.

**What TipRanks should build:**  
A dedicated `/stocks/[TICKER]/insider-transactions/` template for every ticker that includes:
- Net insider activity chart (12-month rolling)
- Notable insider profiles with accuracy history
- Smart Score component breakdown showing insider signal weight
- CTA: "See full insider history — Free account"

**TipRanks' specific differentiator:**  
Insider signal accuracy layer — TipRanks can show whether insiders at *this company* have historically been predictive, which is impossible to replicate without years of proprietary tracking data.
