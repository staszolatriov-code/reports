### Opportunity 1 — "Dividend Stocks with Analyst Buy Ratings" Screener Pages

**Why it matters:**
This is pure purchase-intent traffic. A user searching for "dividend stocks analysts love" or "dividend stocks with strong buy ratings" has already decided they want to invest in dividend stocks — they just want validation and a specific stock pick. This converts to free sign-ups and premium subscriptions at a far higher rate than informational traffic.

StockAnalysis has zero analyst data. Their dividend pages are entirely financial-statement-driven (yield, payout ratio, ex-date). They cannot answer "which dividend stocks do Wall Street analysts recommend buying right now?" This is a structural gap, not a content gap.

**What TipRanks should build:**

**TipRanks' specific differentiator:**

### Opportunity 2 — "Is [Stock Ticker] Dividend Safe?" Individual Stock Pages

**Why it matters:**
Dividend investors' #1 anxiety is a dividend cut. "Is [STOCK] dividend safe?" is a high-intent, low-competition query pattern for hundreds of individual tickers (AT&T, Ford, Altria, Walgreens, etc.) that have historically volatile or high-yield dividends. Each ticker is a standalone page opportunity with long-tail organic traffic that accumulates at scale.

- Templatized per-stock pages: *"Is [TICKER] Dividend Safe? Analyst Ratings & Smart Score Analysis"*
- Sections: Dividend history + payout ratio | Analyst consensus (buy/hold/sell) | Smart Score | Insider activity (buying vs. selling) | News sentiment | Price target vs. current price
- Target the 200–300 highest-yield / most-watched dividend tickers first
- Schema markup for featured snippet eligibility (direct answer at top of page)

**TipRanks' specific differentiator:**
The multi-signal approach (analyst + Smart Score + insider + sentiment) is unique. No other publicly accessible tool synthesizes these inputs into a dividend safety assessment.
