### Opportunity 1 — "Insider Trading Stocks to Buy" Screener Page

**Why it matters:**
This is a high-commercial-intent query with consistent monthly search volume. Users arriving here are ready to act; they convert to premium at a high rate because the product directly addresses their need (finding actionable trade signals).

**Why StockAnalysis is beatable:**
StockAnalysis displays raw SEC Form 4 filings with minimal enrichment — name, ticker, shares, date. There is no historical accuracy layer, no signal confidence score, no integration with price performance after the trade. It is a data dump, not a signal tool.

**What TipRanks should build:**
A dedicated, indexable screener page at `/insider-trading/` (or as an SEO-optimized landing state of the existing screener) that surfaces:
- Insiders with a verified track record (measured by % of trades that outperformed S&P over 3/6/12 months)
- Filter by cluster: C-suite vs. board, purchase size, sector
- A "top insider buys this week" module refreshed daily with structured data (FAQ schema + ItemList schema)

**TipRanks' specific differentiator:**
TipRanks already tracks insider accuracy historically. This is a proprietary moat. No competitor can show that a specific CFO's stock purchases have beaten the market in 8 of the last 10 trades — TipRanks can. That claim, surfaced on the page and in meta descriptions, is the conversion hook.
