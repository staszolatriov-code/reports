**TipRanks' specific differentiator:**
No competitor can replicate the Smart Score + analyst rating + insider buying trifecta on a live dividend screener page. **Why StockAnalysis is beatable:**
StockAnalysis shows payout ratio, earnings history, and free cash flow — raw inputs — but never synthesizes a safety verdict. Simply Financial and Seeking Alpha have some qualitative coverage but no systematic, ticker-level, SEO-indexable safety score. The SERP is wide open.

A "Dividend Safety" feature page that explains TipRanks' methodology (Smart Score components that correlate with dividend continuity: analyst consensus on earnings, insider ownership, financial health grade).
