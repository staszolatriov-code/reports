## MarketBeat.com

### 1. Summary of What Changed

- **Analyst Ratings & Profiles overhaul (Sep 2025, still rolling forward):** MarketBeat rebuilt its analyst ratings pages and launched dedicated analyst profile pages. Each profile shows performance metrics (success rate, average return), coverage focus, ratings distribution, photo, and LinkedIn link. Brokerage-level pages (e.g., Benchmark) were updated as recently as Oct 16, 2026 and show trailing 12-month stats (1,054 ratings, 439 companies).
- **Analyst Ratings Screener:** Filterable by rating action, previous/new rating, and price-target upside. Auto-updates on filter change. Advanced filters and firm rankings gated behind All Access.
- **Premium content push:** Homepage now features a "10 Stocks Set to Soar in Fall 2026" premium report—indicating a conversion-first content strategy overlaid on free SEO pages.
- **Traffic trajectory:** ~7.48M visits (Aug 2026 Semrush estimate), down ~1.82% MoM. Google accounts for 29.68% of traffic—a significant organic share. Direct at 44% reflects strong brand/email moat.

### 2. Why It Matters

Analyst ratings pages are a core TipRanks battleground. MarketBeat has now built a two-tier moat here: (a) free long-tail pages ranking for "[analyst name] track record" and "[brokerage] stock ratings" queries, and (b) a gated screener pushing All Access upgrades. The individual analyst profile pages are a direct competitor to TipRanks' analyst page product—a historically differentiated TipRanks asset. If MarketBeat's analyst pages begin outranking TipRanks on branded analyst queries, it erodes a core conversion path.

### 3. Threat Level

| Finding | Threat Level |
|---|---|
| Analyst profile pages with track record data | **High** |
| Analyst ratings screener (free + gated tiers) | **Medium** |
| Premium content layered on SEO pages | **Medium** |
| Traffic dip (-1.82% MoM) | **Low** (may be seasonal) |

### 4. Recommended Response

- **Strengthen analyst pages:** Audit TipRanks analyst profile pages against MarketBeat's new template. Ensure TipRanks leads on depth: sector-level accuracy, price target history charts, portfolio-level performance.
- **Improve internal linking:** Add links from stock pages → analyst profiles → ratings history to create a stronger crawl path than MarketBeat's setup.
- **Add product modules:** Surface analyst consensus modules on earnings, dividend, and ETF pages to cross-link TipRanks' differentiated data.
- **Monitor analyst profile rankings:** Set up rank tracking for high-value analyst name queries (e.g., "Dan Ives analyst rating," "Cathie Wood track record").

## StockAnalysis.com

### 1. Summary of What Changed

- **Earnings Call Transcripts (Oct 23, 2026):** Each earnings call now has its own indexed URL (`/stocks/{ticker}/transcripts/{id}/`) with full text, audio, one-line AI summary, and linked filings. All major stocks covered (GOOGL, META, AAPL, AMZN, etc.). This is the single biggest SEO move of the monitoring period.
- **Revenue Segment & Geography Breakdown (Oct 19, 2026):** Revenue pages now include segment and geography splits with pie charts for last 3 fiscal years, LTM, and latest quarter. Full history requires Pro.
- **Adjusted PE Ratio (Oct 7, 2026):** New non-GAAP P/E data point added to stock pages for analyst-covered stocks.
- **Financials Overview page (Jul 2026):** A new tab combining profits, segments, cash, cash flow, margins, dividends, and valuation in one chart-heavy page.
- **Fair Value + DCF Calculators (Sep 18, 2026):** Two new tool pages. DCF pre-fills from stock data; users adjust assumptions. Targets "fair value [stock]" and "DCF calculator" queries.
- **Fund Holdings Overlap tool (Sep 23, 2026):** ETF/mutual fund comparison tool with weight, count, sector, and country overlap. Dedicated page + tutorial page.
- **FCF Payout Ratio (Sep 20, 2026):** Added to screener, tables, watchlist, and statistics page—broadening screener keyword surface.
- **Traffic:** 8.38M visits (Jan 2026 Semrush), organic from Google ~20%. Organic reportedly declining MoM.

### 2. Why It Matters

**Earnings transcripts are the critical threat.** Each transcript is a new, crawlable, long-form page targeting "[Company] [Quarter] earnings call transcript" queries—some of the highest-intent queries in financial search. StockAnalysis is building a transcript archive that will compound in indexing value every quarter. TipRanks does not appear to have equivalent transcript pages at scale.

The revenue segment and financials overview pages deepen the data moat on stock financial pages, competing directly with TipRanks' stock analysis pages. The DCF and Fair Value calculators target a new user intent layer (valuation tools) that TipRanks currently doesn't own. The FCF payout ratio expansion broadens the screener keyword surface—StockAnalysis' screener is already a significant organic traffic source.

### 3. Threat Level

| Finding | Threat Level |
|---|---|
| Earnings call transcripts (new page type, compounding) | **Critical** |
| Revenue segment/geo breakdown + Financials Overview | **High** |
| DCF + Fair Value calculator pages | **High** |
| Fund Holdings Overlap tool | **Medium** |
| Adjusted PE ratio + FCF payout ratio data points | **Medium** |
| Fiscal AI as default data source (5-year free) | **Medium** |

### 4. Recommended Response

- **Build earnings transcript pages immediately.** This is a compounding SEO asset. Even AI-enhanced summaries with key quotes, analyst guidance, and stock reaction data would differentiate TipRanks. Prioritize S&P 500 and high-traffic tickers first.
- **Launch a valuation tool page.** A Fair Value or analyst-consensus-based target page targets the same queries as StockAnalysis' DCF calculator, but with TipRanks' unique analyst data as the differentiator.
- **Deepen financials on stock pages.** Segment revenue, geography breakdowns, and FCF metrics should be visible on free TipRanks stock pages—not just behind a paywall—to compete for the organic queries StockAnalysis is capturing.
- **Match screener data points.** Track which metrics StockAnalysis adds to its screener and ensure TipRanks' screener offers equivalent or superior coverage.

## Cross-Competitor Signals

Both competitors are making the same strategic bet: **expand the number of indexed page types and deepen data per page to capture long-tail queries at scale.** StockAnalysis is doing it more aggressively with new page templates (transcripts, segment revenue, calculators). MarketBeat is doing it through data enrichment of existing templates (analyst profiles, screener upgrades).

Neither is retreating behind paywalls—both use free content as the SEO funnel and gate advanced features (full history, advanced screener filters) for conversion. This is a freemium SEO moat strategy.

**Implication for TipRanks:** The window to own earnings transcript SEO is now. StockAnalysis launched this in late October 2026; they will have Q3 2026 earnings season fully indexed within weeks. TipRanks has a differentiated asset (analyst consensus, price targets) that could make its transcript pages more valuable—but only if they exist.

## Priority Action List

1. **[URGENT] Launch earnings call transcript pages.** Target at least the S&P 500 universe before Q4 2026 earnings begin. StockAnalysis will compound this advantage every quarter it goes unmatched.

2. **[HIGH] Audit analyst profile pages vs. MarketBeat.** Ensure TipRanks analyst pages lead on depth: accuracy score, sector performance, price target chart history. Run rank tracking for top analyst name queries.

3. **[HIGH] Build a Fair Value / Analyst Consensus valuation tool page.** Targets "fair value [ticker]," "is [stock] overvalued" queries. TipRanks can differentiate via analyst price target consensus rather than a generic DCF.

4. **[MEDIUM] Add revenue segment and geography data to free stock pages.** StockAnalysis is making this a free-tier feature (recent years only). Matching it prevents TipRanks from losing rankings on "[stock] revenue breakdown" queries.

5. **[MEDIUM] Strengthen internal linking from stock pages → analyst pages → ratings history.** Both competitors are building denser internal link graphs. A stronger crawl path improves indexation depth and passes authority to long-tail analyst and rating pages.
