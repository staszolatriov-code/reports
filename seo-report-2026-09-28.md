## 3. Top 5 Highest-Impact Opportunities

### Opportunity 1: "What stocks are hedge funds buying" cluster

**Why it matters:**  
This single query cluster captures the highest-intent retail investors who are explicitly seeking institutional validation before buying. Search volume is sustained and spikes 40–60% each quarter after 13F deadlines (mid-Feb, mid-May, mid-Aug, mid-Nov). These users convert to premium at above-average rates because they are actively making buy decisions.

**Why StockAnalysis is beatable:**  
StockAnalysis shows raw 13F ownership tables — number of shares, value, change — with no signal layer. There is no fund quality ranking, no trend analysis, no correlation with forward returns. The pages are data dumps, not insights.

**What TipRanks should build:**  
A dedicated, dynamically updated "Hedge Funds Are Buying" hub with: (1) top stocks by fund accumulation trend this quarter, (2) filter by Smart Score ≥ 7, (3) fund confidence tier (top-tier funds only), (4) visual trend lines per stock. This page must live at a clean URL like `/hedge-funds/buying/` and be updated automatically after each 13F filing cycle.

**TipRanks' specific differentiator:**  
Fund-level confidence scoring based on historical fund return accuracy — TipRanks knows which funds have the best track records. "What are the best-performing hedge funds buying?" is a query no competitor can answer with data-backed confidence scores.

**Why StockAnalysis is beatable:**

**TipRanks' specific differentiator:**
