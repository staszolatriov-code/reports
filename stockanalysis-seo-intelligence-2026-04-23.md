# StockAnalysis.com SEO Intelligence Report
**Date:** April 23, 2026
**Prepared for:** TipRanks SEO / Product / Content Team
**Primary Subject:** StockAnalysis.com — Competitor Monitoring

---

## Executive Summary

StockAnalysis.com is executing a disciplined, product-led SEO strategy that is quietly expanding its footprint across every major keyword cluster that TipRanks competes in. Their approach: build deeply functional free tools, scale programmatic pages at massive breadth (130,000+ stocks globally), and gate just enough behind Pro to monetize. Their stock screener now has **299 filters updated once per minute**. Their ETF screener has **107 metrics**. They launched a dedicated Tools hub with calculators. They are moving into portfolio tracking via **brokerage sync (beta)**. They've built IPO screeners, year-by-year IPO list pages, press release aggregation, and "how to invest" educational articles.

TipRanks still holds stronger brand recognition and analyst-rating differentiation — but StockAnalysis is winning on data breadth, tool depth, and free-tier generosity. Several of their moves require immediate TipRanks response.

**StockAnalysis global rank: ~4,564** vs **TipRanks global rank: ~5,917–6,056** (Similarweb).

## 1. Summary of What Changed

### 1.1 Tools Hub Launched (`/tools/`)
StockAnalysis created a dedicated **Tools index page** housing standalone calculators including:
- **Dividend Calculator** (`/tools/dividend-calculator/`) — projects dividend income with/without DRIP reinvestment
- **CAGR Calculator** (`/tools/cagr-calculator/`) — calculates compound annual growth rate from any start/end values

These are standalone, linkable, indexable pages targeting high-volume informational queries like "dividend calculator," "CAGR calculator," and "investment growth calculator."

### 1.2 Stock Screener Expanded to 299 Filters
The stock screener at `/stocks/screener/` now supports:
- **299 screening filters**, updated as frequently as once per minute
- New filters: **WACC** (Weighted Average Cost of Capital), **Float %**, **Beta (1Y and 2Y)**
- New date filters: **52-Week High Date**, **52-Week Low Date**, date range selectors
- **CAGR filters (1Y–20Y)** for annualized return screening
- **Dividend Growth 3Y & 10Y** as CAGR metrics
- Indicator modals with "i" icons showing metric definitions inline
- Ability to create watchlists directly from screener results

### 1.3 ETF Section Deepened
- ETF Screener (`/etf/screener/`) expanded to **107 indicators and metrics**
- ETF Comparison Tool (`/etf/compare/`) with saved comparisons dropdown
- **ETF Holdings pages** now include information boxes, stats, and breakdown charts for non-US ETFs and mutual funds
- **Sector / Asset Class / Country tabs** added to US ETF holdings pages
- **ETF Overview performance widget** added (example: SPY)

### 1.4 IPO Section Scaled Programmatically
StockAnalysis is systematically building a large programmatic IPO content cluster:
- `/ipos/` — 200 most recent IPOs
- `/ipos/calendar/` — Upcoming IPO calendar
- `/ipos/screener/` — IPO screener with filtering
- `/ipos/statistics/` — Annual IPO charts and stats from 2000–2026
- `/ipos/2025/` and `/ipos/2026/` — Year-specific IPO list pages (auto-updating)

As of April 2026, they track 102 US IPOs year-to-date.

### 1.5 Brokerage Sync (Beta) — Portfolio Tracker Entry
Pro users can now **connect a brokerage account and sync holdings** into a portfolio tracker. This is a strategic product expansion beyond pure data research into **active portfolio management workflows** — the same territory as TipRanks' Smart Portfolio.

### 1.6 Real-Time Streaming Data (Pro)
Pro tier now includes **real-time streaming chart data** for all US-listed stocks and ETFs, including pre-market and after-hours quotes. This closes the gap with Bloomberg/Schwab-tier tools for active users.

### 1.7 Comparison Tool Enhancements
- Stock Comparison Tool at `/stocks/compare/` now allows **saved comparisons**
- ETF Comparison Tool at `/etf/compare/` similarly updated
- These tools directly target "AAPL vs MSFT," "[ETF1] vs [ETF2]" comparison queries — a growing high-intent keyword cluster

### 1.8 Sector and Industry Pages (`/stocks/industry/`)
- **11 sectors** and **145 industries** each have their own landing pages
- Sectors sortable by market cap, PE ratio, profit margin, performance
- This is a massive programmatic keyword surface for "[industry] stocks" queries

### 1.9 Article / Educational Content Expansion (`/article/`)
- StockAnalysis has begun publishing long-form "how to invest in X" articles (e.g., "45 Ways to Invest in AI")
- These are indexable `/article/` URLs targeting informational + commercial investigation queries
- This signals a push into content marketing and top-of-funnel SEO that directly competes with TipRanks editorial

### 1.10 Press Releases Hub (`/news/press-releases/`)
A dedicated press release aggregation page from publicly traded companies, pulling from Globe Newswire and Business Wire — building content freshness signals and news entity coverage across all tickers.

### 1.11 Financial Data Quality Improvements
- Financials pages received an **improved layout** for table customization
- **ROIC formula updated** to a more accurate version: `ROIC = (Operating Income × (1 − Tax Rate)) / Average Invested Capital`
- **Employee pages** now show quarterly data by default, with full-time/part-time columns
- **Stock Exchanges list page** redesigned with new filtering and sorting

### 1.12 Screener Tutorial Page
A dedicated tutorial page at `/tutorials/screener/` was created — building SEO coverage for "how to use stock screener" queries and improving user onboarding.

## 2. Why It Matters

### 2.1 Tools Hub → High-Volume Calculator Queries
**Ranking risk:** HIGH. "Dividend calculator," "CAGR calculator," and similar queries have 10K–100K monthly searches. StockAnalysis now has dedicated, tool-first pages with strong brand authority. TipRanks does not have a standalone calculator suite.
**Business risk:** MEDIUM-HIGH. Calculator tools are top-of-funnel acquisition pages. Users who discover StockAnalysis via calculator tools have a clear conversion path to their screener → Pro subscription.
**Should TipRanks respond?** Yes, immediately.

### 2.2 299-Filter Screener → Screener Query Dominance
**Ranking risk:** HIGH. The screener at once-per-minute update frequency is a flagship SEO and product moat. Queries like "best stock screener," "free stock screener," and long-tail filter queries ("stocks with high dividend growth") funnel to StockAnalysis first.
**Business risk:** HIGH. Users who choose StockAnalysis for screening are unlikely to use TipRanks for the same workflow — screener loyalty is sticky.
**Should TipRanks respond?** Yes — add screener filters, promote TipRanks' Smart Score filter as a unique differentiator.

### 2.3 Brokerage Sync → Portfolio Management Territory
**Ranking risk:** MEDIUM. This is less about SEO and more about product moat.
**Business risk:** HIGH. Brokerage sync creates deep daily-use engagement that dramatically increases retention. If TipRanks' Smart Portfolio doesn't match this, retention risk is real. Users won't maintain two portfolio trackers.
**Should TipRanks respond?** Yes — accelerate brokerage sync stability and promote it aggressively in onboarding.

### 2.4 IPO Programmatic Pages → IPO Query Cluster
**Ranking risk:** HIGH. Year-specific IPO list pages (`/ipos/2026/`, `/ipos/2025/`) auto-update and rank for high-volume queries like "2026 IPOs," "upcoming IPOs," "IPO calendar." StockAnalysis has a structured multi-page IPO cluster that is hard to dislodge once established.
**Business risk:** MEDIUM. IPO pages attract early-stage investors and new users — a valuable acquisition cohort.
**Should TipRanks respond?** Yes — audit TipRanks IPO page coverage and build out the year-page + screener pattern.

### 2.5 Sector/Industry Pages → "[Industry] Stocks" Queries
**Ranking risk:** HIGH. 145 industries × multiple metrics = massive long-tail surface area. Queries like "semiconductor stocks," "biotech stocks by market cap," "best healthcare stocks" are captured programmatically.
**Business risk:** MEDIUM-HIGH. These pages build brand awareness among investors researching sectors — the same users TipRanks targets.
**Should TipRanks respond?** Yes — build sector/industry landing pages if not already present with comparable depth.

### 2.6 Article Content Expansion → Informational Query Coverage
**Ranking risk:** MEDIUM-HIGH. StockAnalysis entering "how to invest in X" territory signals a deliberate shift toward capturing top-of-funnel queries. This directly competes with TipRanks editorial and blog content.
**Business risk:** MEDIUM. These articles build brand trust and create internal linking pathways to high-intent pages (screener, stock pages, ETF pages).
**Should TipRanks respond?** Monitor and match — TipRanks has a content advantage with analyst data; it should double down on analyst-backed educational content.

### 2.7 ETF Coverage Depth → ETF Investor Acquisition
**Ranking risk:** HIGH. With 107 ETF screener metrics, ETF comparison tools, and per-ETF holdings breakdowns with sector/asset class tabs, StockAnalysis is the dominant free ETF research tool. Queries like "QQQ vs SPY," "best dividend ETFs," "ETF screener" are at risk.
**Business risk:** MEDIUM-HIGH. ETF investors are a high-LTV segment. TipRanks' ETF screener must match data depth.
**Should TipRanks respond?** Yes — benchmark TipRanks ETF pages against StockAnalysis and identify gaps.

## 6. Strategic Signals

### What StockAnalysis Is Prioritizing
StockAnalysis is executing a **three-layer SEO strategy**:

- **Layer 1 — Data breadth at scale:** 130,000+ stocks globally, 145 industries, year-specific IPO pages, press releases — all programmatic, all freshness-optimized, all targeting long-tail queries.
- **Layer 2 — Tool depth as moat:** 299 screener filters, 107 ETF metrics, standalone calculators, comparison tools — these create high-engagement, bookmark-worthy experiences that build direct traffic and suppress competitor visibility.
- **Layer 3 — Product expansion to defend retention:** Brokerage sync, real-time streaming, watchlist from screener — transforming from a research site into an investment workflow platform.

### What This Implies About Their SEO Strategy
1. They are deliberately compressing TipRanks' addressable keyword surface from multiple directions simultaneously — screeners, calculators, IPO pages, sector pages.
2. The 14.38% month-over-month traffic drop (Nov 2025) suggests they may have faced a Google core update hit, but their structural depth gives them strong recovery potential.
3. Their free-tier generosity (no paywall on most data) is a deliberate top-of-funnel strategy to win organic traffic first, monetize via Pro conversion second.
4. The article content expansion signals they now want to own informational + commercial investigation queries, not just ticker-direct queries.

### Where TipRanks Can Counterattack
1. **Analyst ratings as a moat no competitor can replicate.** StockAnalysis has price targets and peer comparisons — but not TipRanks' depth of analyst track record data, Smart Score, or Financial Blogger rankings. These should be the centerpiece of every stock page, screener, sector page, and article.
2. **Calculators with analyst-powered forecasts.** A dividend calculator that uses analyst dividend estimates is materially better than a generic calculator. This is a defensible product-SEO differentiator.
3. **News sentiment and hedge fund data as unique modules.** StockAnalysis does not have insider trading tracking, hedge fund holdings trends, or blog analyst rankings at TipRanks' depth. These modules should be more prominent and more linkable.
4. **Global analyst coverage.** TipRanks' global exchange coverage with analyst data is a differentiated asset. Build dedicated pages for major non-US stocks with analyst data that StockAnalysis cannot match.
