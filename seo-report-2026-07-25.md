### Opportunity 2 — Analyst Price Target Tracker Pages (Per Ticker)

**Why it matters:**  
"[TICKER] price target," "[TICKER] analyst price target," and "[TICKER] stock forecast" are among the highest-volume ticker-specific queries in investing search. **TipRanks' specific differentiator:**
