## 3. Top 5 Highest-Impact Opportunities

### #1 — Analyst Ratings Pages at Scale (Ticker-Level)

**Why it matters:**
"[TICKER] analyst ratings" queries account for tens of millions of annual searches across all publicly traded stocks. These are high-intent users actively researching a stock before making a buy/sell decision — the exact moment a free TipRanks account converts.

**Why StockAnalysis is weak here:**
StockAnalysis surfaces a basic consensus label (Buy/Hold/Sell) and average price target. It does not show analyst-level detail, individual analyst accuracy, track record by stock type, or historical rating changes. The page is thin and does not answer the follow-up question: *"Is this analyst actually worth listening to?"*

**What TipRanks should build:**
- Ensure every major ticker page (S&P 500, Russell 2000 + mid-cap growth) is fully indexed, fast, and structured with schema markup for `AggregateRating`, `FinancialProduct`, and `FAQPage`.
- Add an above-the-fold summary card: consensus, average price target, upside %, analyst count, and the top analyst's accuracy score and star rating.
- Embed a 12-month analyst rating history chart — interactive on desktop, static image with alt text for SEO.
- Surface the best-performing analyst covering the stock with a link to their profile.

**TipRanks' specific differentiator:**
Analyst accuracy scores and star ratings are 100% proprietary. No other site can show "This Buy rating comes from a 5-star analyst with 72% success rate" — making TipRanks the only source that adds *quality* signal to consensus data.
