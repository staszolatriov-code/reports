## 3. Top 5 Highest-Impact Opportunities

### Opportunity 1 — "What Stocks Are Hedge Funds Buying" Hub Page

**Why it matters**  
This is the highest-volume, highest-intent query in the hedge fund space. It drives quarterly traffic spikes around 13F filing windows (mid-Feb, mid-May, mid-Aug, mid-Nov) and sustains baseline search volume year-round. Users asking this question are actively seeking investment ideas — the shortest path to a premium trial.

**Why StockAnalysis is beatable**  
StockAnalysis renders raw 13F tables with no scoring, no ranking, and no cross-reference to analyst consensus or price targets. The page is a data dump, not a decision tool. It ranks on domain authority alone, not content quality.

**What TipRanks should build**  
A dynamically updated hub page that shows the top 20–30 stocks currently held by the most hedge funds, filtered by Smart Score ≥ 7, with columns for: number of funds holding, QoQ change in position, average analyst consensus, and price target upside. Update on each 13F cycle. Include a "see all" CTA behind a free sign-up gate.

**TipRanks' specific differentiator**  
Smart Score overlay on institutional holdings — no other platform correlates hedge fund conviction with analyst accuracy and insider sentiment in a single ranked view.
