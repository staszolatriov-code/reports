## MarketBeat.com

### 1. Summary of What Changed

- **Analyst Ratings & Profile Overhaul (Sept 2025):** Major upgrade to analyst pages. New individual analyst profile pages now include success rates, average returns, best/worst historical calls, coverage focus areas, ratings distribution (bullish/bearish/neutral tendency), analyst photos, and LinkedIn profiles. Based on 750,000+ tracked recommendations.
- **13F Filings + Congressional Trading Tools:** New dedicated content section at `/congress-stock-trades/` covering buying/selling by members of Congress, filterable by time, state, and trade size. Separate 13F holdings analysis pages also launched.
- **Dividend Content Depth:** "50 Best Dividend Stocks for 2026" list page live; dedicated dividend screener and ex-dividend calendar with their own URL structure clearly targeting transactional dividend queries.
- **Homepage & Help Section Refresh:** New homepage template and improved help section launched, signaling broader UX template investment.
- **Earnings Screener:** Dedicated earnings screener at `/all-access/earnings-screener/` with custom filtering.

### 2. Why It Matters

The analyst ratings upgrade is a **direct product-level assault on TipRanks' core value proposition**. TipRanks built its brand on analyst ranking and track-record data — MarketBeat now offers a comparable free layer. The congressional trading and 13F tools tap into high-intent, viral queries that generate strong backlinks and social sharing. Dividend content (50 best stocks, screener, calendar) targets high-conversion investor queries TipRanks is not dominating. Traffic gap is severe: MarketBeat sits at ~100M visits vs. TipRanks' ~2.76M.

### 3. Threat Level

| Finding | Threat Level |
|---|---|
| Analyst profile pages (performance metrics, track records) | **Critical** |
| Congressional trading + 13F tool pages | **High** |
| Dividend content cluster (screener, calendar, best stocks list) | **High** |
| Earnings screener | **Medium** |
| Homepage/template refresh | **Medium** |

### 4. Recommended Response

- **Analyst profiles:** Immediately audit TipRanks' own analyst pages vs. MarketBeat's new template. TipRanks must own this SERP — refresh title tags, add structured data (Person schema), deepen visible performance stats above the fold without a login wall.
- **Congressional trading:** Build or refresh a congressional trading page with TipRanks' data angle (combine with Smart Score signals). This query cluster is a link magnet.
- **Dividend cluster:** Commission a "Best Dividend Stocks" editorial page, improve dividend screener SEO, and strengthen internal linking between dividend-related pages.
- **Earnings screener:** Improve conversion hooks on TipRanks' earnings pages; add ungated previews.

## StockAnalysis.com

### 1. Summary of What Changed

- **Reverse ETF Lookup Tool:** New tool at `/tools/etf-reverse-lookup/` — enter a stock ticker, get all ETFs holding it with weighting and expense ratios. Captures high-intent "which ETFs hold [TICKER]" long-tail queries at scale.
- **Filings Page (New Content Type):** New `/stocks/[ticker]/filings/` pages aggregating 10-Ks, quarterly reports, earnings call slides, proxy statements, and investor relations docs. This is a new content category.
- **Tools Index Page + CAGR Calculator:** New `/tools/` hub page with multiple calculators (CAGR calculator live). Captures financial tools queries; natural backlink destination.
- **Fiscal AI as Default Data Source:** Switched default financials data to Fiscal AI for US/Canadian stocks (S&P Global still switchable). Signals data quality differentiation play.
- **Screener Upgrades:** Added 52-Week High/Low Date fields, Profitable Quarters filter (screen for companies just turning profitable), and saved screener setups. ETF screener now includes date range picker.
- **Brokerage Syncing (Beta):** Portfolio brokerage sync for Pro users — retention tool that deepens session engagement signals.
- **Comparison Tool Improvements:** Saved comparisons dropdown + date range slider added to stock/ETF compare tools.

### 2. Why It Matters

StockAnalysis is executing classic **product-led SEO**: each new tool page targets a specific query cluster, generates backlinks organically, and reduces bounce rate by serving the full research journey on-site. The Filings page is particularly dangerous — it creates a direct content layer for "AAPL 10-K," "TSLA earnings slides" type queries that generate significant organic traffic and keep users in-session. The Tools hub is a top-of-funnel SEO play: calculators rank well, earn links, and convert visitors to registered users. StockAnalysis traffic at ~8.3M monthly remains well above TipRanks at 2.76M.

### 3. Threat Level

| Finding | Threat Level |
|---|---|
| Filings page (new content type, 10-K / earnings slides queries) | **High** |
| Reverse ETF Lookup Tool (long-tail ETF query capture) | **High** |
| Tools hub + CAGR calculator (top-of-funnel SEO) | **Medium** |
| Profitable Quarters screener filter (niche investor queries) | **Medium** |
| Brokerage sync (engagement / retention signal) | **Low** |
| Fiscal AI data source switch | **Low** |

### 4. Recommended Response

- **Filings:** TipRanks should audit whether its stock pages link to or surface SEC filings. If not, add a filings tab or module to stock pages — even a curated, annotated view would differentiate vs. StockAnalysis's raw aggregation.
- **ETF Reverse Lookup:** Build a TipRanks version with Smart Score overlay (e.g., "Which ETFs hold the highest-rated stocks?"). This creates a unique angle, not a copy.
- **Tools hub:** Identify 2-3 calculator or tool pages TipRanks can own — dividend yield calculator, analyst consensus calculator, or portfolio Smart Score aggregator. Publish and internally link from stock pages.
- **Screener depth:** Match "Profitable Quarters" type filters. Ship at least one unique screener filter per quarter to maintain competitive parity.

## Cross-Competitor Signals

Both MarketBeat and StockAnalysis are independently converging on the same playbook:

1. **Product-led SEO:** Every new feature gets its own indexable URL. Tools, calculators, and lookup pages are SEO entry points, not just UX features.
2. **Institutional data democratization:** Congressional trades, 13F filings, SEC filings — data formerly behind paywalls is now free content driving organic traffic.
3. **Screener deepening:** Both are adding niche filters that capture long-tail screener queries and improve perceived data quality.
4. **Comparison tools:** Stock and ETF comparison pages are being actively improved — they rank for "[Stock A] vs [Stock B]" queries that have high commercial intent.

**What this means for TipRanks:** Competitors are commoditizing TipRanks' data moat from below (free tiers) while attacking its highest-value queries (analyst ratings, earnings, dividends) from above. TipRanks' Smart Score is still a genuine differentiator, but it is not surfaced aggressively enough in SEO contexts. The gap between TipRanks' ~2.76M monthly visits and MarketBeat's ~100M is a structural threat that worsens with each product expansion by competitors.
