### Opportunity 1: "What Are Hedge Funds Buying?" — Quarterly 13F Hub Page

**Why it matters:**  
This is the single highest-volume query cluster that spikes immediately after each 13F deadline (February, May, August, November). The August 2026 cycle is active right now. Queries include: "what stocks are hedge funds buying," "most popular hedge fund stocks," "hedge fund buys Q2 2026," "top hedge fund holdings 2026." Collectively these represent tens of thousands of high-intent monthly searches from exactly the kind of investor TipRanks wants — active, data-hungry, premium-conversion-ready.

**Why StockAnalysis is weak/beatable:**  
StockAnalysis.com presents 13F data as raw filing tables per fund. There is no aggregation across funds, no ranking by conviction level, no freshness signal on the page, and no connection to analyst consensus or price targets. The UX dead-ends at a filing list. TipRanks can offer: ranked "most-bought stocks by hedge funds this quarter," filtering by sector/market cap, and each stock card showing Smart Score + analyst consensus + hedge fund count all in one row.

**What TipRanks should build:**  
A dynamically updated hub page titled "Stocks Hedge Funds Are Buying — Q3 2026 13F Report" that aggregates holdings across 500+ tracked funds, ranks by new positions + added shares, and shows Smart Score inline. Refresh four times per year on 13F deadline + 2 weeks. Include top fund movers (biggest new buys, biggest sell-offs) and sector breakdowns.

**TipRanks' specific differentiator:**  
Hedge fund conviction score combined with Smart Score and analyst consensus in one view — no competitor has all three signals on one page. A free user can see the top 5 stocks; unlocking the full list requires a free account (email capture) or premium.

### Opportunity 3: Insider Trading Tracker — Live Tool Page

**Why it matters:**  
"Insider trading tracker," "insider buying stocks," "CEO stock purchases," and related queries are evergreen and convert extremely well — insiders buying their own stock is one of the clearest bullish signals retail investors understand intuitively. This cluster also attracts financial journalists and bloggers, generating organic backlinks. Monthly search volume is significant and consistent, not seasonal.

**Why StockAnalysis is weak/beatable:**  
StockAnalysis has an insider transactions section but it is a static table of recent filings, sorted by date. No filtering by significance (large buys vs. small), no signal scoring, no connection to stock performance after insider buys, no sector-level aggregation.

**What TipRanks should build:**  
A live "Insider Trading Tracker" page that functions as a proper tool: filterable by date range, sector, transaction size, insider type (CEO/CFO/Director), and buy/sell. Each row shows the insider name, transaction details, and — uniquely — the stock's Smart Score and analyst consensus at the time of the trade. A "Performance of Recent Insider Buys" section showing average return 30/60/90 days post-buy makes TipRanks the definitive destination.

**TipRanks' specific differentiator:**  
Backtested insider buy performance data combined with Smart Score. TipRanks can show that insider buys where Smart Score ≥ 8 outperformed insider buys where Smart Score < 5 by X% — a proprietary insight no competitor can replicate.
