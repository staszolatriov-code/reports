### Opportunity #1 — "Hedge Funds Buying [Ticker]" Programmatic Pages

**Why it matters:**  
Every retail investor wants to know if smart money is behind a stock they're watching. Searches like "hedge funds buying NVDA," "which hedge funds own AAPL," and "institutional buying [stock]" are high-intent, mid-funnel queries directly tied to investment decisions. This is one of the fastest-growing search clusters in retail investing, accelerating as 13F filing culture becomes mainstream on social platforms.

**Why StockAnalysis is beatable:**  
StockAnalysis surfaces 13F data in a clean table — fund name, shares held, change — but stops there. No signal scoring. No convergence with analyst consensus. No narrative layer. The page answers "who owns it" but not "what does this mean." Users bounce to find interpretation elsewhere.

**What TipRanks should build:**  
On every stock page (and as standalone SEO landing pages for the top 500 tickers), add a dedicated "Hedge Fund Activity" module featuring: (1) net change in HF ownership over the last 2 quarters, (2) notable funds entering/exiting, (3) convergence badge — when HF buying aligns with a top-rated analyst BUY and a bullish Smart Score, surface a "Smart Money Confluence" signal. Create standalone pages: `tipranks.com/stocks/[ticker]/hedge-funds` optimized for `[ticker] hedge fund ownership` queries.

**TipRanks' specific differentiator:**  
The Smart Score × institutional ownership convergence is a proprietary, defensible signal. When TipRanks can say "NVDA has a Smart Score of 9, is being bought by 47 hedge funds this quarter, and top-rated analysts are Bullish" — that is a product experience, not just data. No competitor can replicate this. The "Smart Money Confluence" concept should be a named, branded feature.
