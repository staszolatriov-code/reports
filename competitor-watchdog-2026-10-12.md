## MarketBeat.com

### 1. Summary of What Changed
- **Backlink erosion:** Semrush August 2026 data shows backlinks dropped **-12.1%** while referring domains rose +5.05% — the site is losing link depth even as new domains trickle in.
- **AI Idea Engine:** Premium-gated AI stock-picking tool now embedded in All Access ($249/year). Every Monday a free "Top Pick" is pushed to free users as a conversion hook.
- **Analyst Ratings expansion:** Per-brokerage pages (`/ratings/by-issuer/[firm]`) now cover dozens of firms with buy/hold/sell counts and gated brokerage accuracy rankings. Upgraded profiles per a Sept 2025 announcement.
- **Compare Stocks tool** (`/compare-stocks/`) allows up to 10-stock side-by-side comparisons including analyst forecasts — a high-intent comparison page template that scales across tickers.
- **Sector comparison pages** in sitemap (biotech, bank, airline, etc.) — passive SEO inventory targeting sector-level queries.
- **"Biggest Losers" content** — dynamic pages by month, quarter, year live in sitemap as of Sept 30, 2026.
- **Overall traffic:** ~14.7M visits/month (SimilarWeb Aug 2026), bounce rate 64.55%, ranked #33 in Investing.

### 2. Why It Matters
The backlink decline is a signal of softening domain authority momentum — but MarketBeat is offsetting it with product-led content (per-brokerage pages, comparison tools, AI hooks). The Compare Stocks template is dangerous: it creates hundreds of scalable comparison pages capturing transactional queries like "AAPL vs MSFT" directly in search. The AI Idea Engine freebie creates a weekly indexed landing page (`/stocks/monday-pick/`) that generates fresh content and email list growth simultaneously.

### 3. Threat Level
| Finding | Threat Level |
|---|---|
| Compare Stocks template (scalable comparison pages) | **High** |
| Per-brokerage analyst pages capturing branded queries | **Medium** |
| AI Idea Engine + Monday Pick as conversion funnel | **Medium** |
| Backlink decline (-12.1%) | **Low** (opportunity for TipRanks) |

### 4. Recommended Response
- **Build new page:** TipRanks needs a scalable stock comparison template (`/compare/AAPL-vs-MSFT/`) with analyst consensus, Smart Score differential, and price target gaps. MarketBeat already has this; TipRanks does not rank for these queries at scale.
- **Strengthen internal linking:** Link from individual stock pages to a comparison CTA ("Compare with competitors").
- **Add product modules:** Surface TipRanks Smart Score prominently on any comparison or analyst page — it's a differentiator MarketBeat lacks.
- **Monitor:** Track MarketBeat's backlink recovery over 60 days; if it continues declining, aggressively target its ranking keywords with fresh content.

## StockAnalysis.com

### 1. Summary of What Changed
- **TSP Fund Pages** (live by Oct 6, 2026): Individual pages for all Thrift Savings Plan funds (F, S, C, I, G funds) with live prices, returns, and expense ratios. Targets ~6M+ federal employees and their retirement search queries.
- **Reverse ETF Lookup tool** (July 7, 2026): Enter any stock ticker → see every ETF holding it. Tutorial page created simultaneously for SEO. Unique tool with no direct competitor equivalent.
- **Help Center** (Aug 26, 2026): Searchable articles, video walkthroughs, browsable categories. Creates hundreds of indexable informational pages around "how to use" queries.
- **Tutorial pages:** Dedicated tutorial pages for the ETF Reverse Lookup and Fundamental Chart — low-competition informational pages driving top-of-funnel traffic.
- **Analysts page: "Most Followed" tab** (Aug 27, 2026): Ranks Wall Street analysts by user follow count — a social proof / engagement signal that builds return visits (Pro-gated).
- **Trending: "Most Alerted" tab** (Aug 24, 2026): New engagement signal next to Most Viewed.
- **App update (Oct 6):** Europe screener filter + earnings calendar filter added to mobile.
- **Traffic estimate:** ~8.1M visits (SimilarWeb Aug 2026); Ahrefs organic ~1.5M/month (May 2026).

### 2. Why It Matters
StockAnalysis is executing a textbook **product-led SEO expansion**: each new tool creates indexable pages at scale (every stock × ETF lookup = thousands of result pages; each TSP fund = new template capturing niche but high-intent queries). The Help Center is particularly dangerous as a long-tail SEO play — it creates "how to analyze [X]" informational content that TipRanks doesn't have. The TSP pages tap a 6M-person audience (federal workers) whose retirement search behavior is almost entirely uncaptured by TipRanks.

### 3. Threat Level
| Finding | Threat Level |
|---|---|
| TSP fund pages targeting federal employee investors | **High** |
| Reverse ETF Lookup — unique tool with SEO moat | **High** |
| Help Center = hundreds of new informational pages | **Medium** |
| Tutorial pages capturing "how to" informational queries | **Medium** |
| Most Followed analyst tab (engagement + retention) | **Low** |

### 4. Recommended Response
- **Build new page:** TipRanks should build TSP fund pages immediately. The template already exists (fund overview, performance, holdings). This is a greenfield segment — no analyst ratings exist, so TipRanks should lead with news, holdings, and performance data.
- **Build new page:** An "ETF Exposure" feature on every stock page (which ETFs hold this stock) would directly counter the Reverse ETF Lookup. This is achievable as a product module.
- **Improve template:** Add "How to use TipRanks" tutorial-style content pages. The Help Center play is a real SEO gap — TipRanks should have indexed help/tutorial pages for core feature queries.
- **Add product modules:** Surface analyst consensus on comparison pages; embed Smart Score as the primary differentiator to capture users who landed from informational content.
- **Monitor:** Track StockAnalysis TSP and ETF Lookup pages for ranking velocity over 60 days.

## Cross-Competitor Signals

**Both competitors are converging on product-led SEO:** every new tool generates new URL templates at scale (per-ticker, per-fund, per-comparison). This is the dominant SEO growth lever in the financial data space and TipRanks must match it.

**Analyst ratings pages are arms-raced:** MarketBeat (per-brokerage), StockAnalysis (Most Followed tab), and TipRanks (Smart Score) are all fighting for the same "analyst ratings" keyword cluster. TipRanks's differentiation must be clearly communicated in title/meta/H1 — "Best Analyst Accuracy" is a defensible angle neither competitor owns explicitly.

**Informational content is being mined:** StockAnalysis's Help Center and tutorial pages signal a pivot into top-of-funnel informational SEO that supplements their data pages. MarketBeat already runs editorial content. TipRanks is behind in informational/educational content at scale.

**AI Overviews headwind affects all three:** Industry data (Pew Research cited Aug 2026) shows an ~8–38% CTR reduction on queries where AI Overviews appear. Financial data queries are highly fact-based and increasingly AI-answered. All players need structured data (schema markup, clear numerical data tables) to appear as AI Overview citations rather than being displaced.
